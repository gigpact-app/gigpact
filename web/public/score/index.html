Watch a worker get paid, step by step. Each key is one thing that can happen to the money once the fee is locked. Press Play and follow the stage.

Press Play the performance, or play a key.

  • proven on mainnet, real wallet
  • ran on mainnet by script, test key
  • test chain only, not on mainnet
  • joy: the worker is paid
  • sad: the money goes back
  • tension: a dispute
  • two voices meet: a split
  • time decides

Listen: bright and rising when the worker is paid, soft and falling when the money goes back, tense when there is a dispute. The key that is playing lights up in its colour.

From the keyboard: 1 and 2 for Accept, 3 to 6 for After the event, 7 to 0 for Claim stage, then Q W E R T Y for Dispute.
