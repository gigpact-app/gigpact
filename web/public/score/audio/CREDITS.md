# Music credits

The 20 clips in this folder were composed with AI assistance (Claude) for The Gig Score,
a BCH BLAZE 2026 project, and rendered to audio with the GeneralUser GS SoundFont
by S. Christian Collins. The SoundFont itself is not included here, only the rendered audio.

Each clip has a feeling that matches the path it belongs to: joy when the worker is paid,
sadness when the money goes back to the organizer, tension in a dispute.
